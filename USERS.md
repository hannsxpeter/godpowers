# Users and Community

**Godpowers has no recorded production users.**

That line stays at the top until it is no longer true. A tool whose purpose is
refusing to let software claim more than it can prove does not get to make an
exception for its own marketing.

## What changed in 7.0

The 6.x line grew to 124 commands, most of which asked a strong model to do
what it already does well. Version 7 keeps the parts that still earn their keep
(state, recorded checks, review, a security pass, a ship gate), moves
enforcement into hooks, and drops the rest.

## The proof we still need

One real project, on a codebase nobody here knows, taken to a shipped result
with Godpowers, and the same work done without it. The A/B harness
([docs/ab-eval.md](docs/ab-eval.md)) exists for that comparison. If you run it,
please share the numbers, especially the ones that make Godpowers look bad.

Record at least:

- the repository shape and the tasks,
- the model and effort level for both arms,
- tokens, cost, and wall-clock time,
- how often you had to step in, and why,
- what the independent review found,
- whether you would ship each result.

## Using it on something real?

Open an issue or a discussion with what worked and what did not. The second half
is more useful than the first.
