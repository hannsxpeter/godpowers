# Responsible Use

Use this extension only for content you own or are authorized to process.

## Appropriate uses

- Remove unnecessary provenance metadata from your own files before sharing.
- Clean invisible Unicode that breaks search, diffs, parsing, or copy and paste.
- Inspect how supported text and file provenance signals behave in authorized research.
- Create a private cleaned copy of your own draft when policy permits it.

## Inappropriate claims and uses

- Do not use a cleaned result to make a false claim of human authorship.
- Do not use the extension to bypass a required disclosure, platform rule, contract, or applicable law.
- Refuse mutation requests whose stated purpose is disclosure evasion, assessment cheating, false-authorship preparation, or policy bypass.
- Do not describe missing metadata as proof that a file was never AI-assisted.
- Do not process content when you lack ownership or authorization.
- Do not reuse a remote or internal-network grant for an origin other than the exact origin the user approved.

## Reporting standard

Always separate:

1. Deterministic and verifiable removals reported by the service.
2. Best-effort rewriting or pixel regeneration that can change content.
3. Residual risk that this extension cannot test or remove.

Residual risk includes C2PA soft binding, audio and video watermarks, secret-key
detectors, unavailable official vendor detectors, and model-training backdoors.
A technical cleanup does not change authorship history or disclosure duties.
