- [DECISION] Modify `skills/god-review.md`, `skills/god-build.md`, `specialists/god-executor.md`, `specialists/god-quality-reviewer.md`, and `references/orchestration/GOD-ORCHESTRATOR-RUNBOOK.md` to consume the protocol and apply conditional wide-change review.

### Module Boundaries

- [DECISION] The shared reference owns evidence meanings, boundary classes, impact policy, proof procedure, output sections, and verdict rules.
- [DECISION] `/god-review` and `/god-build` own bounded-versus-wide Stage 2 orchestration without changing their public command names or the two-stage public contract.
- [DECISION] `god-executor` proposes one candidate safety fact and focused proof but never grades its own work.
- [DECISION] `god-quality-reviewer` independently verifies or replaces the candidate, matches executed proof to the existing ledger, and owns the Stage 2 safety-case verdict.
- [DECISION] `lib/impact.js` owns static candidate discovery only, while `lib/evidence.js` remains the executed record authority and adds only digest-bound event metadata plus a read-only sanitized review resolver.
- [DECISION] Feature awareness advertises the stronger existing review behavior, and no new command, route, workflow, specialist, dependency, or evidence store is introduced.

### Public Contracts

- [DECISION] `/god-review` remains a two-stage review command and adds a Stage 2 safety case with one load-bearing safety fact, 10 boundary rows, evidence level 1 through 5, Confirmed Risks, Cleared Risks, Unproven Claims, and Before Merge.
- [DECISION] Evidence below level 4 is labeled `UNPROVEN`; high-impact `UNPROVEN` claims fail Stage 2, and lower-impact claims warn with one exact next proof.
- [DECISION] A change is wide when it crosses at least 3 boundary classes or 2 high-impact classes, and a wide Stage 2 runs at least 2 independent blast-radius passes in fresh contexts before reconciliation.
- [DECISION] `impact.forArtifactDiff` and `impact.forDesign` retain their current fields and add an `evidence` object with `kind: static-candidate`, `status: unproven`, `maximumLevel: 2`, and an explanatory limitation.
- [DECISION] Existing `godpowers verify` claim, command, substep, exit, timestamp, and freshness behavior remains unchanged and is cited by level 4 and level 5 conclusions.

### Call And Data Flow

1. [DECISION] The executor reads the approved slice and shared protocol, captures the boundary classes implicated by the diff, and returns one candidate safety fact plus the smallest focused proof command.
2. [DECISION] Stage 1 verifies specification, acceptance criteria, traceability, and scope without accepting the executor's safety conclusion.
3. [DECISION] Stage 2 independently scans all 10 boundaries, verifies or replaces the candidate fact, computes the wide threshold, and requests a second fresh-context pass when the threshold is met.
4. [DECISION] Each pass resolves level 4 or level 5 proof through a local sanitized projection that verifies the fresh ledger record, its digest-bound gate event, and the event chain rather than trusting terminal narration or passing raw output to the reviewer.
5. [DECISION] The Stage 2 reconciliation keeps confirmed, cleared, and unproven conclusions separate, lowers confidence on disagreement, and never turns reviewer consensus into proof.
6. [DECISION] `lib/impact.js` attaches static-candidate metadata to its affected-file results so `/god-design-impact` and Stage 2 cannot present linkage or basename-search output as executed Godpowers evidence.
7. [DECISION] Feature awareness and documentation describe the strengthened existing review surface after focused tests and independent review pass.

### Reused Patterns

- [DECISION] Reuse the shared-reference pattern under `references/building/`, existing specialist required-context declarations, and the two-stage reviewer handoff instead of adding a command or agent.
- [DECISION] Reuse `lib/evidence.js` claim records and `npx godpowers verify` rather than adding a safety-case database or proof command.
- [DECISION] Reuse `lib/events.js` chain verification and additive gate-event attributes rather than adding a signing service, secret key, or second authority.
- [DECISION] Reuse additive result metadata from other runtime helpers so current `lib/impact.js` consumers remain compatible.
- [DECISION] Reuse `scripts/test-harness.js`, explicit test-runner registration, package-content guards, feature-awareness refresh, and the current release pipeline.

### Non-Goals

- [DECISION] Do not add a slash command, CLI operation, route, recipe, workflow, specialist, hosted service, production dependency, evidence store, or state writer.
- [DECISION] Do not change the existing verification record shape, promote static search to proof, let the executor grade itself, or let reviewer agreement increase an evidence level.
- [DECISION] Do not claim that a local hash chain authenticates against an actor that can rewrite the entire trusted workspace and recompute every event.
- [DECISION] Do not require runtime reproduction for a fully deterministic local transformation or when no evidenced runnable target exists.
- [DECISION] Do not copy or lightly rewrite upstream pstack prose, code, fixtures, examples, or output formats.
- [DECISION] Do not tag or publish until the release candidate is merged to `main` and passes the complete release and prepublication gates.
